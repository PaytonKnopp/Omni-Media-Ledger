Quick tips

A few pointers

Everything worth knowing to use this app well, in one place.

Cards & tiering
🖱️ Click any card's title or body to expand it and see all ~19 stats, not just the 3 shown up front.
🥇 The row under each card lets you mark Gold / Silver / Bronze favorites or "owned" in one click — no need to expand first. Gold is your absolute favorite, Silver the next tier down, Bronze the one after that — this tiering is the single thing that drives your ★ GOAT Match score and recommendations everywhere in the app. That row always sits at the bottom of the card, so it lines up card to card no matter how long a title is or whether you're in compact or comfortable view.
🏷️ Genre, vibe, format and platform tags inside an expanded card are read-only — just information, like the stat bars next to them.
🔗 "Cross-medium pairings" and "If you like this, cross over to" inside an expanded card are clickable — click one to jump straight to it in the Global Controller search.
Filtering & sorting
📌 Pin any slider you use a lot (Advanced Filters, or the ≥ sliders themselves) so it shows up in the always-visible row up top instead of staying tucked away. Both the pinned row and Advanced Filters start in alphabetical order.
🔀 The Sort dropdown is grouped by category — Reception, Craft & Fidelity, Mood & Feel, Depth & Ideas — with each group alphabetized, so related sorts sit together instead of one long list.
Collection
◆ Mark what you own from the tier row on any card, then head to the Collection tab to manage it.
💿 Document the exact edition you own — DVD / Blu-ray / 4K for movies & TV, Hardcover / Softcover for books — right from the Collection tab. The ⬆ Upgrade Audit reads this to suggest real upgrades instead of guessing.
▤ Group by Series and the 📚 Shelf View both list your collection alphabetically, so a series or a shelf reads the same way every time.
Discovery
🕳 Rabbit Hole and 🎲 Surprise Me pull a weighted pick for you — by mood, medium, ownership, or how much time you have.
★ The GOAT Profile tab has a full search to declare Gold/Silver/Bronze favorites directly, plus your generated taste breakdown and recommendations.
Accounts & your profile
☁️ Sign into an account (top right) to sync your picks across devices — type any name; the same name anywhere brings it all back. Every profile — tiers, owned formats, pinned sliders, theme, everything — is remembered per account.
⬇ Export / Import / Reset (top right, "Profile") back up your whole profile to a file, load one back in, or start blank.
One more thing
💡 See something missing? Use "Suggest a feature" in the header, top of page. Click the ? button next to the theme selector anytime to bring these tips back.
